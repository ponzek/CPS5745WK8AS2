About Voldemort

Learn about the origins and rise of Tom Marvolo Riddle, better known as Lord Voldemort.

Tom Marvolo Riddle

Born to the witch Merope Gaunt and the Muggle Tom Riddle Sr., young Tom grew up in a London orphanage. A gifted but cold child, he was invited to Hogwarts by Albus Dumbledore and sorted into Slytherin House.

Obsessed with power and terrified of death, he rearranged the letters of his name into a new identity: I am Lord Voldemort.

"There is no good and evil, there is only power, and those too weak to seek it."
