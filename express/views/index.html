Introduction

Discover the life and times of Lord Voldemort, the Dark Lord — the wizard so feared that most dare not speak his name.

"He Who Must Not Be Named"

The Dark Side

Lord Voldemort

Born Tom Marvolo Riddle, heir of Slytherin and leader of the Death Eaters.

Lucius Malfoy

Wealthy pure-blood Death Eater who slipped Tom Riddle's diary to Ginny.

Draco Malfoy

Harry's Slytherin rival, ordered by Voldemort to kill Dumbledore.

Dolores Umbridge

Pink-clad Ministry official who ruled Hogwarts with blood quills and decrees.

Dumbledore's Army

Harry Potter

The Boy Who Lived, and the one destined to defeat the Dark Lord.

Hermione Granger

The brightest witch of her age, key to hunting down the Horcruxes.

Ron Weasley

Loyal friend who destroyed Slytherin's Locket with the Sword of Gryffindor.

Ginny Weasley

Fierce Gryffindor who survived the Chamber of Secrets and fought at Hogwarts.

Neville Longbottom

Herbology whiz who stood up to Voldemort and slew Nagini, the final Horcrux.

Luna Lovegood

Dreamy Ravenclaw who helped find Ravenclaw's Diadem.

Hogwarts Staff

Albus Dumbledore

Headmaster of Hogwarts and the only wizard Voldemort ever feared.

Minerva McGonagall

Transfiguration professor and Head of Gryffindor who led Hogwarts' defense.

Severus Snape

Potions master and double agent whose loyalty was a secret until the end.

Rubeus Hagrid

Keeper of Keys and Grounds, and the man who told Harry he was a wizard.

Argus Filch

Grumpy Squib caretaker forever chasing students out of bounds.

Mrs Norris

Filch's watchful cat, petrified by the Basilisk in Chamber of Secrets.
