History of Voldemort

Explore the dark history of Lord Voldemort, from his early years to his rise to power.

1926 — Tom Riddle is born in a Muggle orphanage in London.
1938 — Begins at Hogwarts and is sorted into Slytherin.
1943 — Opens the Chamber of Secrets and creates his first Horcrux.
1970s — Leads the Death Eaters in the First Wizarding War.
1981 — His killing curse rebounds off baby Harry Potter.
1995 — Regains a body in the Little Hangleton graveyard.
1998 — Defeated for good at the Battle of Hogwarts.
